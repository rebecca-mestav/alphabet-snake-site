Ocean Planet

The ocean is thousands of water particles. Gravity pulls them toward the planet; they push apart when packed too tightly and can cling together a little. The top layer is joined into the water surface, splashes that break away get their own surface, and lone drops show as white foam. Small waves ride on the surface: the boat and the fish start them and they spread out in rings. Drag the planet to turn it (a click stops it): your finger pushes the water aside.
