Alphabet Snake

A word game for Android, computers and iPhone.

Play in your browser

Drag your finger to steer a hungry snake around the board. Every letter it eats joins its body, and when the letters spell a word, you score. Longer and rarer words are worth more, and the clock is always ticking.

  • Play solo against the clock, or race a friend or the computer in Versus matches.
  • Play in English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Turkish or Japanese.
  • Tap any word to see what it means.
  • See global high scores, the best words anyone has found, and Versus win rates, shared between the Android app and the browser version.
  • On an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen.
