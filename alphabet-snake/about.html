Alphabet Snake

A word game by Beckuro Games, for Android and web browsers.

Alphabet Snake is a word game. Drag your finger across the board and a hungry snake follows it, eating letters as it goes. Let go to swallow the word: real words score points and add time to the clock, and made-up words lose points. The background turns green when you are carrying a real word, and rainbow when it can't get any longer.

What you can do

  • Play quick solo games against the clock, in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish or Japanese.
  • Look up the meaning of every word you find, and see the longer words you could have made.
  • Play Versus matches against the computer or other players online.
  • Collect hats and other cosmetics for your snake.
  • Compare your best games and words with other players on global high score lists.

Signing in

You can play without an account. Signing in with Google (or Google Play Games on Android) is optional: it lets you choose a username for the global lists and keeps your word lists and cosmetics backed up, so you can carry on from another phone or browser. Alphabet Snake only uses your Google account to sign you in. Your email address is never shown to other players. See the privacy policy for details.

Play

Play Alphabet Snake in your browser. No account or download needed.
