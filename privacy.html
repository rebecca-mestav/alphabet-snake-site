Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Alphabet Snake app ("the game") uses and why. The game is made by Bec, an independent developer. If you have any questions, email beckurogames@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no ads and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • Your scores, favourite words and settings are kept on your own phone.
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games, the username you choose, your scores and the words you find are sent to the game's online lists so other players can see them.
  • You can ask for your online information to be deleted at any time.

Information kept on your phone

The game saves your high scores, the words you have found, your favourite words and your settings (such as language, music and sound volume) on your device. This stays on your phone and is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Google Play Games sign-in

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name from Google, and your best scores are posted to the game's Play Games leaderboards. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy.

Online high scores and word lists

When you are signed in, the game uses Google Firebase (a service run by Google) to keep global lists. To do this it stores:

  • the username you choose, linked to your Play Games player ID;
  • your game scores and the words in each game, with the date;
  • which words you were the first to find, and how many times you have eaten each word;
  • your Versus match totals (wins, losses and draws);
  • a random ID that Firebase creates for your copy of the game, used to keep other people from changing your records.

Your username, scores, words and Versus win rate are shown publicly to other players of the game. Please don't use your real name or personal details as your username.

Online Versus matches

When you play a match online, the game uses Firebase Realtime Database to share the match with the other player: a match code, the random Firebase ID of each player, and the moves and letters in the match. This information is only used to run the match.

Permissions

The game uses internet access for online features and vibration for game effects. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Who processes your information

Online information is stored by Google (Firebase and Google Play Games Services) on the game's behalf and is sent over encrypted connections. You can read how Google handles it in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. It is not shared with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Online scores and word records are kept while the game's global lists are running. To have your username and your online records deleted, email beckurogames@gmail.com with your username and the subject "Delete my data", and they will be removed within 30 days. You can also stop sending information at any time by signing out of Play Games or uninstalling the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created a username, email us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
