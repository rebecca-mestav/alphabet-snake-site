Amigurumi Round Builder

Type a pattern in the round. Each stitch is a node joined to the stitch it's worked into, and yarn tension settles the piece.

Start from an example

Rounds use sc, hdc, dc, tr, dtr, trtr, qtr, inc, dec (US terms; heights 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 sc). Change yarn with a line like color #c0392b, or as an item inside a row. fasten off ends the yarn; join into st 40 (or join into side of st 40) starts new yarn there. flo and blo work into one loop (flo sc around, sc blo into st 12); a top stays one point until both its loops are used. A stitch can go through another piece's top too: sc into st 40 + Fin st 36 (make that piece first). Start with R1: 6 sc in MR, or with a chain like R1: ch 11 for flat rows. Repeats like (sc 2, inc) x6, ranges like R7-12: sc 36, and sc around all work. A count in brackets at the end, like (18), gets checked. After the rounds, a stitch-by-stitch row says exactly where each stitch goes: R13: 2 sc into st 40, sc into st 41 + st 42, sc into side of st 12. Rows can also hold sl st into st 9 (no height, so sl st into st 8 + st 9 pulls two tops together) and ch 3 (new stitches on a strand, worked into like any other). Stitches are numbered in the order they're made. Skipped stitches are left open: the next stitch makes one triangle between the previous stitch's top, its own top and the stitch it's worked into. A slip stitch or join makes its top and what it's worked into one point. Chains next to a dc (2 chains) or tr (3 chains) form fabric with it. Other pieces go between piece Wing and end piece lines, numbered from st 1. sew Wing st 3 to st 40, Wing st 4 to 0.5 st 41 + 0.5 st 42 makes each pair one point, so the line between consecutive sewn points is shared by both pieces (add , around to close the loop). eye at st 120 adds a safety eye.

Work the next stitch

Click a stitch top in the 3D view, or a stitch side. The next stitch along the spiral is picked for you. Stitches you work are added to the current row of the pattern; press End row to start the next one.

Pieces, sewing and safety eyes

Save the main piece to use it again. Adding a saved piece puts it beside this one, in its own piece Name … end piece block. To sew, turn on Sew mode, click a stitch on the added piece, then click anywhere on the piece it goes onto. Repeat for each point, then press Sew: the gap between one point and the next is closed with fabric. A safety eye goes at the stitch you've picked.
